Schengen Gate Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026.

This policy covers Schengen Gate Buddy — the app named Gate Buddy on your Home Screen — published by Game Pac (Panduranga Shenoy Kudige). Gate Buddy is a free app that counts days under the Schengen 90/180 rule from the dates you enter. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. Questions about this policy or about your data are welcome at kudige@icloud.com.

What the app never does

Gate Buddy has no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app contains no third-party SDKs or other third-party code. This version of the app has no location access: it never asks for your location, your photos or your contacts, and it makes no network connections of its own.

Links in the app to the EU's own tool for checking how long you can stay open in your browser, and nothing about you is passed to it.

What is stored on your device

Everything you enter — your passport's country and your answer to its one question, your trips, your visas, your long-stay permits and any note you type — is saved in one file in the app's own folder on your device. Your settings — such as whether first run is done and which reminders are on — are kept in the app's settings on your device. Passport numbers, visa sticker numbers, your location and your photos are never asked for and never stored. These records are not sent to any developer server, because there is none.

Apple's device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include the app's data file and settings. Apple manages those backups and their retention under your iCloud and device settings; restoring a backup can restore your trips. The developer has no access to your backups.

You can delete any trip, visa or permit inside the app. Deleting the app removes its data file and settings from the device; data already inside an Apple backup is governed by Apple's backup settings.

Reminders (optional)

Reminders are off until you switch them on in Settings. If you switch reminders on, the app asks iOS for permission to show notifications and then schedules them on your device. They are local notifications: nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing leaves your phone.

Export and import

Export writes your data to a JSON file that you choose where to save or share; import reads back a JSON file you pick and replaces what the app holds only after you confirm. The file goes only where you send it; exported copies stay wherever you saved them.

TestFlight beta testing

If you install the app through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects beta crash and usage information and shares it with the developer; this platform collection cannot be opted out of, and it can include tester and device details. Feedback you deliberately submit through TestFlight, such as comments or screenshots, is voluntary and is shared with the developer. This Apple platform collection is separate from the app itself, which adds no tracking of its own. For details, see Apple's official notice, TestFlight & Privacy.

Support emails and developer-held data

Emailing support is optional. If you write to kudige@icloud.com, your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include are used to help with your request.

Support emails held by the developer, and TestFlight reports and feedback downloaded by the developer, are used only for support and app improvements. They are kept only as long as needed for those purposes and then deleted, unless longer retention is legally required. Copies held by Apple follow Apple's own privacy policy. To ask about access to or deletion of developer-held data, contact support; deletion of Apple's own copies cannot be promised, as those are governed by Apple.

About these web pages

Opening these pages uses ordinary web hosting. These pages themselves add no tracking, ads, analytics, or forms. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical requests as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be dated on this page. Questions or requests can be sent to kudige@icloud.com, and the Support page has more contact details.
